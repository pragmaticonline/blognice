Privacy Policy

blognice is operated by Pragmatic Online Co., Ltd., Thailand.
Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what we collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you. Questions can be sent to privacy@blognice.com.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers account holders who create and manage blogs, and readers or subscribers who visit a blognice-powered blog or subscribe to its email updates.

What we collect

Account holders

  • Email address, for sign-in and service communications.
  • Password hash. Passwords are not stored in readable form.
  • Blog content, images, audio, settings, domains, and profile details, to operate and publish the blog.
  • Hashed API-key identifiers, to authenticate API requests.
  • Billing and payment metadata. Card details are handled by Stripe; crypto payments are handled by NOWPayments.

Readers and subscribers

  • Email address and a random subscription/unsubscribe token when someone subscribes.
  • Subscription confirmation and delivery status, to prevent abuse and send only requested messages.

Analytics and technical data

Public pages may use a random first-party visitor identifier in browser local storage to distinguish returning visits. We derive and store an external referring hostname, not the full referring URL. Events may include page path, approximate country, broad device category, browser family, and audio-play events. Blog owners receive aggregate reports, not visitor email addresses or raw identifiers. We do not sell this data or use it for behavioral advertising.

Where prior consent is required, analytics does not start until you choose “Allow analytics.” You can choose “Decline analytics” or later open “Analytics preferences” to withdraw. Withdrawal removes the local identifier and stops future optional analytics events. This regional decision uses Cloudflare’s approximate request-country signal and can be affected by VPNs, travel, or network routing.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure the service, publish blogs, process subscriptions and payments, send requested transactional or blog notifications, provide aggregate analytics, provide support, prevent abuse, and comply with law. Depending on the jurisdiction and purpose, the legal basis may be contract, legitimate interests, consent, or legal obligation.

Blog owners are responsible for the content they publish and for determining whether subscriber communications comply with applicable law. blognice provides the tools and processes data on their behalf where applicable.

Cookies and local storage

The admin uses an opaque, secure session cookie for signed-in account access. Public pages use local storage for theme preference and, where permitted, a random analytics visitor identifier. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare Workers, D1, R2, Analytics Engine, CDN, custom-domain infrastructure, and Workers AI.
  • Stripe, for card subscriptions and hosted billing management.
  • NOWPayments, for annual prepaid cryptocurrency payments.
  • MailNice, for transactional and subscriber email delivery.
  • Resend, only where configured as an email fallback.

These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices. We share only what is needed for the relevant feature and do not sell personal information. AI prompts, post text, and selected image context are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI only when an account holder requests image or audio generation.

International transfers

Pragmatic Online Co., Ltd. is based in Thailand. Providers may process information in Thailand, the EU, the United States, or other countries where they operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as contractual protections.

Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal and accounting requirements, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and maintain security. Raw Analytics Engine reporting is available for up to 90 days; aggregate archives, backups, cached copies, and legal records may remain longer where necessary.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or information about processing. Every subscriber email includes unsubscribe controls. Send requests to privacy@blognice.com; we aim to respond within 30 days or any shorter period required by law.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures including memory-hard password hashing, hashed API keys, HTTPS, access controls, and non-public databases. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Report suspected vulnerabilities to security@blognice.com.

Children

blognice is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will update the date above and, where required, give account holders reasonable notice of material changes.
