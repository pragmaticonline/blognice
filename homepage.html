How it works

From a new account to a blog on your own domain

01

Create your account

Choose a blog address, a title, and a password. You land straight in the editor, ready to write your first post.

02

Write, preview, publish

Draft in Markdown, check the Preview tab, then publish. The page renders on the server and is ready to read right away.

03

Connect your domain

When you're ready, add a domain you own from your dashboard. It shows you exactly which DNS records to add.

Blognice vs WordPress

Simple publishing, versus general-purpose website management

Choose WordPress when you need

  • An online shop with a large product catalogue
  • A page builder with complex, custom layouts
  • Membership or community features
  • Extensive third-party integrations
  • A website that does far more than publish articles

Choose Blognice when you want

  • A calm, focused place to write and publish
  • A simple Markdown editor, not a page builder
  • Fast pages without performance tuning
  • Up to five blogs under one simple account
  • No plugins, updates, or hosting to manage

WordPress gives you everything you might possibly need.
