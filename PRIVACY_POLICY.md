# Privacy Policy

**blognice** is operated by Pragmatic Online Co., Ltd., a company registered in Thailand.

**Last updated: August 2026**

This policy explains what we collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you. Questions can be sent to [privacy@blognice.com](mailto:privacy@blognice.com).

## Who this policy covers

This policy covers account holders who create and manage blogs, and readers or subscribers who visit a blognice-powered blog or subscribe to its email updates.

## What we collect

### Account holders

- Email address, for sign-in and service communications.
- Password hash. Passwords are not stored in readable form.
- Blog content, images, audio, settings, domains, and profile details, to operate and publish the blog.
- Hashed API-key identifiers, to authenticate API requests.
- Billing and payment metadata, such as provider customer IDs, plan, payment/order IDs, currency, amount, status, and entitlement dates. Card details are handled by Stripe; crypto payments are handled by NOWPayments.

### Readers and subscribers

- Email address and a random subscription/unsubscribe token, when someone subscribes to a blog.
- Subscription confirmation and delivery status, to prevent abuse and send only requested messages.

### Analytics and technical data

On public pages, a random first-party visitor identifier is stored in browser local storage. We use it to distinguish returning visits, not to identify a person by name or follow them across unrelated sites. Our browser code receives the referring-page URL to derive the external referring hostname; we store the hostname, not the full referring URL, in our analytics dataset. Events may also include the page path, approximate country, broad device category, browser family, and audio-play events. Blog owners receive aggregate reports and do not receive visitor email addresses or raw identifiers.

We do not use this data for behavioral advertising or sell it.

In regions where we determine that prior consent is required, analytics does not start until you choose “Allow analytics.” You can choose “Decline analytics” or later open “Analytics preferences” to withdraw; withdrawal removes the local identifier and stops future optional analytics events. This regional decision uses Cloudflare's approximate request-country signal and may be affected by VPNs, travel, or network routing.

## How we use information

We use information to provide and secure the service, publish blogs, process subscriptions and payments, send requested transactional or blog notifications, provide aggregate analytics, provide support, prevent abuse, and comply with law. Depending on the jurisdiction and purpose, the legal basis may be contract, legitimate interests, consent, or legal obligation.

Blog owners are responsible for the content they publish and for determining whether their subscriber communications comply with applicable law. blognice provides the tools and processes data on their behalf where applicable.

## Cookies and local storage

The admin uses an opaque, secure session cookie for signed-in account access. It contains no readable personal information and expires after 30 days or when the session is revoked. Public pages use local storage for theme preference and a random analytics visitor identifier. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

## Service providers

Depending on the features used, information may be processed by:

- Cloudflare Workers, D1, R2, Analytics Engine, CDN, custom-domain infrastructure, and Workers AI, for hosting, storage, analytics, image generation, and audio generation.
- Stripe, for card subscriptions and hosted billing management.
- NOWPayments, for annual prepaid cryptocurrency payments and payment-status notifications.
- MailNice, for transactional and subscriber email delivery.
- Resend, only where configured as an email fallback.

These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices. We share only what is needed for the relevant feature and do not sell personal information.

If an account holder uses AI image or audio features, we send the relevant prompt, post text, and/or selected image context to Cloudflare Workers AI to generate the requested output. Generated media and associated content may be stored in Cloudflare R2 and published at the account holder's direction.

## International transfers

Pragmatic Online Co., Ltd. is based in Thailand. Providers may process information in Thailand, the EU, the United States, or other countries where they operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as contractual protections. Provider-specific transfer terms may also apply.

## Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal and accounting requirements, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and maintain security. Account and published content may remain until deletion is requested or the account is closed. Subscriber records remain until unsubscribe, removal, or blog deletion. Payment and tax records may be retained for the period required by law.

Raw Analytics Engine reporting is available for up to 90 days. Daily page-view and event aggregates may be retained beyond 90 days under our retention practices. Cached copies, backups, and legal records may take longer to expire or may need to be retained.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or information about processing. We may verify your identity and may limit a request where law permits or requires retention. Every subscriber email includes unsubscribe controls.

Send requests to [privacy@blognice.com](mailto:privacy@blognice.com). We aim to respond within 30 days, or within the shorter period required by applicable law. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. In Thailand, this is the Personal Data Protection Committee; in the EU/UK, it is generally your national supervisory authority.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including memory-hard password hashing, hashed API keys, HTTPS, access controls, and non-public databases. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Report suspected vulnerabilities to [security@blognice.com](mailto:security@blognice.com).

## Children

blognice is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will assess the request under applicable law.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will update the date above and, where required, give account holders reasonable notice of material changes.
