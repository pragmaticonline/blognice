Commission at a glance

Rate50% of Eligible Revenue (after discounts/taxes/refunds/credits, before provider fees) — per qualifying payment, half-up rounding
Qualifying paymentsMonthly: first 12 non-zero monthly charges  ·  Annual: first non-zero annual charge. Prorations/upgrades excluded in v1.
Example$36/yr annual at 10% off → $32.40 eligible × 50% = $16.20.   $5/mo monthly at 10% off → $4.50 × 50% = $2.25 per month.
CurrencyUSD only in v1. Non-USD retained but $0 commission until FX policy ships.
AttributionOpens a 60-day opportunity for unpaid, unattributed accounts. Code entered takes precedence only if no attribution exists. Immutable once set. Closed at first checkout.

Disputes reserve commission, refunds create negative entries. Blognice records are authoritative (subject to correction). Full policy in Affiliate Terms §§2–4.

Payouts

  • Provider: Stripe Connect only (Express). Blognice never stores bank details.
  • Schedule: Monthly when net matured USD balance ≥ US$100. Earned commission matures 60 days after provider-confirmed payment.
  • Countries at launch: US CA GB TH — requires Stripe-supported country/corridor for Thailand platform. We may add/remove countries.
  • Adjustments: Refunds/credits/disputes adjust the balance; negative balance can block payouts.
